# Growth Plan — Nomad AI

## ICPs
- Budget-conscious solo travelers (18–35)
- Remote workers / digital nomads
- Travel content creators
- Small group trip planners (friends, couples)

## Positioning
Nomad AI = AI travel companion that builds smart itineraries, reveals hidden gems, and surfaces real-time travel signals — all free to try.

## 5-step GTM
1. Launch on Product Hunt + Hacker News with demo video and live links
2. Twitter/LinkedIn threads with itinerary examples and signals screenshots
3. Partner with 5 travel creators to co-create city guides powered by Nomad AI
4. Discord/Reddit seeding in travel and digital nomad communities
5. Weekly email digest of top 5 deals/events by region — CTA back to app

## Activation loop
- Onboard with 1-click demo (Barcelona)
- Encourage save to Memory Journal
- Prompt to subscribe to weekly digest

## Outreach templates
Cold DM (creator)
```
Hey {name} — built Nomad AI, a free AI travel companion that generates day-by-day itineraries and spotlights real-time travel signals (deals/events). Would love to power a {city} guide with your brand. Happy to co-promote + share insights. Want to try a demo?
```

Cold DM (community)
```
Hi folks — launching Nomad AI, a free AI travel planner + signals feed. It’s great for planning a weekend in {city} or finding hidden gems. Would love feedback. Live demo here: {frontend_url}
```

## Metrics
- WAU/MAU, itinerary completions, memory adds
- Digest subscriptions, CTR
- City-specific engagement and share rate

## Monetization roadmap (later)
- Premium: personal travel model + offline maps, collaborative planning
- Affiliate links for flights/hotels (opt-in)
